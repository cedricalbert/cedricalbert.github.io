Programs at the Université de Moncton

The lab is based at the École de foresterie on the Edmundston campus, close to the Acadian forest and with strong ties to industry partners.

Language. The Université de Moncton is a French-language university. Students need working French for courses, exams, presentations and reports. Research work and publications can be in English if you prefer.

Studying at another university

Research often spans several institutions. Dr. Albert co-supervises MSc and PhD students registered at partner universities, and can help put together a project and a supervisory committee. We currently have collaborators at:

  • Université Laval, Faculté de foresterie, de géographie et de géomatique
  • University of New Brunswick, Forestry and Environmental Management

How to apply

Send an email to cedric.albert@umoncton.ca with:

  1. A short statement of interest: which project or topic, and why
  2. Your CV
  3. Unofficial transcripts
